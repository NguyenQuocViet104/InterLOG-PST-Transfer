INTERLOG PST TRANSFER v1.2 - HUONG DAN NHANH
================================================

1. Giai nen TOAN BO file ZIP tren may user.
2. Bam START-PST-TRANSFER.cmd.
3. Chon PST tren may ao/share va thu muc local tren may user.
4. Bam BAT DAU CHAY NGAM / TU RESUME.

KHI MAT MANG / SMB / VPN:
- KHONG xoa PST dang tai, receipt hoac BITS job.
- Ket noi lai VPN/LAN.
- Mo share bang File Explorer va dang nhap neu Windows yeu cau.
- Mo lai cong cu, chon dung thu muc dich cu.
- Bam KET NOI LAI / RESUME.
- Cho 10-30 giay, bam Kiem tra job nen.

Neu BITS da tai du 100% nhung con hien Transferred, nut KET NOI LAI / RESUME
se tu khoi dong monitor va chot PST thanh COMPLETE.

Chi xoa PST tren may ao sau khi:
- Cong cu bao COMPLETE.
- PST dich co dung kich thuoc.
- Outlook Classic mo duoc PST va IT da kiem tra du lieu.
